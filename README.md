# FitSphere – Gym Management App

FitSphere is a Flutter-based gym management application designed to bring gym operations and member fitness activities into one centralized platform. It helps streamline gym session management, member subscriptions, diet planning, and fitness progress tracking through an intuitive mobile interface.

## Features

- **Gym Session Management:** Organize and manage gym sessions and workout activities.
- **Member Subscription Management:** Handle member subscriptions and keep track of membership details.
- **Diet Management:** Manage diet plans and support members with their nutritional goals.
- **Fitness Tracking:** Monitor members' fitness activities and track their progress.
- **Interactive Dashboard:** Present important gym and fitness information in an organized interface.
- **Data Visualization:** Display fitness-related statistics and progress through interactive charts.
- **State Management:** Manage application state efficiently using Provider.

## Benefits

- Simplifies everyday gym management activities.
- Centralizes member subscriptions and fitness-related information.
- Helps organize workout sessions and diet plans.
- Provides better visibility into member fitness progress.
- Offers an organized and user-friendly mobile experience.
- Reduces the complexity of managing different gym activities separately.

FitSphere combines gym administration and member fitness management into a single mobile application, providing an organized approach to handling gym sessions, subscriptions, nutrition, and fitness progress.
